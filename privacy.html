Privacy Policy – Nuvexo

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Nuvexo respects your privacy and is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Nuvexo application (“Nuvexo,” the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

1. About Nuvexo

Nuvexo is a media player that allows users to access and play media from IPTV or media services selected and provided by the user.

Nuvexo does not provide, host, sell, distribute, or control television channels, movies, series, playlists, subscriptions, or other media content.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Nuvexo may store information locally on your device where necessary to provide App functionality.

This may include:

  • Server or portal addresses
  • Login credentials for user-selected IPTV or media services
  • Playlist information
  • App and player settings
  • Favorites
  • Viewing history and playback progress

This information is stored locally on your device and is not stored on servers operated by Nuvexo.

3. IPTV Credentials

Credentials entered into Nuvexo are used to connect to the IPTV or media service selected by the user.

When required for the App to function, these credentials are transmitted directly from your device to the third-party server or service you have chosen to use.

Nuvexo does not operate those third-party servers and does not control how those providers process information.

4. Viewing Activity

Nuvexo may store viewing history, playback positions, favorites, and similar information locally on your device to provide features such as Continue Watching.

Nuvexo does not collect or store this viewing activity on Nuvexo-operated servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Nuvexo connects only to services and sources selected by the user. These third-party services operate independently from Nuvexo and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services they choose to access through Nuvexo.

6. Technical Information

Nuvexo does not currently operate a tracking service designed to collect your viewing behavior or build a profile of your activity.

The platform, app store, operating system, device manufacturer, or third-party services used with Nuvexo may independently process technical information according to their own policies.

7. Advertising and Sale of Personal Data

Nuvexo does not sell personal information.

Nuvexo does not currently use personal information to create advertising profiles or track users across unrelated apps or services.

If these practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as required.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Nuvexo remains on your device until it is removed through App functionality, cleared through device settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled, depending on the device and operating system.

9. Data Security

Nuvexo is designed to limit unnecessary collection of information. However, no device, network connection, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their device and their third-party service credentials.

10. Children's Privacy

Nuvexo is not specifically directed at children. Nuvexo does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Nuvexo-operated servers.

11. European Privacy Rights

Where applicable, users in the European Economic Area may have rights under data protection laws, including the GDPR.

Because Nuvexo is designed to store relevant App information locally rather than on Nuvexo-operated servers, Nuvexo may not possess personal data that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted on the user's behalf.

Requests concerning data processed by a third-party IPTV or media provider should be directed to that provider.

12. Google Play and Apple App Store

If Nuvexo is downloaded through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another distribution platform, that platform may independently process information related to downloads, purchases, device information, diagnostics, or account activity according to its own privacy policy.

Such processing is controlled by the relevant platform provider and not by Nuvexo.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the “Last Updated” date will be revised.

Continued use of Nuvexo after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgement of the updated policy where permitted by applicable law.
